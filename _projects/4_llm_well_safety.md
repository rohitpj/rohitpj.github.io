At **SLB Cambridge Research**, I finetuned, evaluated and deployed a large language model system intended to improve safety on oil wells, drawing on multi-modal datasets.

The interesting part of the work was less the model than everything around it. A safety context sets a high bar: a system that is usually right is not automatically useful, and evaluation has to be built around the failure cases that actually matter rather than aggregate accuracy. Getting from a finetuned checkpoint to something deployable meant treating evaluation design as a first-class part of the problem.

The system is currently **under invention disclosure**, so I've kept the description here at the level of what's already public.
