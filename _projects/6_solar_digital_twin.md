A **digital twin** of a solar power-based energy storage system, built at **SLB Cambridge Research**.

The model simulates the system closely enough to plan against, which turns two decisions into optimisation problems rather than judgement calls: how the system should be *constructed*, and how it should be *operated* once built. Both plans were optimised jointly against a two-part objective — minimising capital and operating **cost** while minimising **greenhouse gas emissions**.

The two objectives pull against each other in most configurations, so the useful output is not a single plan but the trade-off surface between them, and where along it a given site should sit.
