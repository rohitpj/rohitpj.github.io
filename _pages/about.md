I’m a Computer Science graduate from the University of Bath, where I completed a Masters in Computer Science with First Class honours. My research primarily focuses on optimising Computer Algebra using Machine Learning, and recently I have been exploring technical AI Safety.

My work for [ICMS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-031-64529-7_20) explored using GNN representations for choosing variable orderings in cylindrical algebraic decomposition, and found significant data pollution issues in existing benchmark datasets. More recently, at SCML 2026, I improved Symbolic Integration in the SymPy computer algebra system by using Reinforcement Learning and Transformer models to learn optimal rule selection instead of relying on heuristics or pre-labelled datasets.

Alongside my academic research I spent a year at SLB Cambridge Research, where I worked on LLM finetuning & deployment, developed novel graph partitioning algorithms for rig scheduling optimisation and built a digital twin of a solar energy storage system to optimise cost and emissions. Before that I spent a summer at the University of Exeter, working on Bayesian imputation and Transformer-based forecasting for noisy weather data.

I’m interested in reinforcement learning, computer algebra, combinatorial optimisation, and getting language models to do useful work on structured problems. You can find my [publications](https://rohitpj.github.io/publications/) and [CV](https://rohitpj.github.io/cv/) here, or reach me by email.
